# Third party assets

The model, its rig and its animations in this folder are **not** covered by the MIT license the rest
of MiVertexAnimation ships under. They are CC0, which is what makes them safe to redistribute here.

## KayKit Character Pack

`Rig_Medium_General.fbx` and the clips inside it

- Author: Kay Lousberg — https://kaylousberg.com
- License: **CC0 1.0 Universal** — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- "Free for personal and commercial use, no attribution required."

CC0 asks for nothing, so this file is a courtesy rather than an obligation. It is here so that anyone
reading the repository can see why a non-MIT asset is sitting in it, and where it came from.

`Rig_Medium_GeneralController.controller` and `Rig_Medium_General.prefab` were made for this sample
and carry the package's own MIT license. They hold no asset data of their own — the controller points
at clips inside the FBX, and the prefab is the model with that controller assigned.
